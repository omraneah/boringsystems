# lab/

A side-door experimental track. Quarantined from the rest of the workspace — does not feed go-to-market, does not couple to boringsystems / personal-apps positioning, does not depend on the engagement-shapes signal-check.

## The 10% rule (load-bearing)

This track receives a maximum of **10% of dedicated effort** until something catches up or doesn't.

- The other 90% lives in the existing tracks: relationship-led conversations, the four engagement shapes, the published case-file site, the personal-apps portfolio.
- 10% is approximately ~1 hour per evening, time-boxed.
- "Until something catches up" = either the probe clears the success floor (see `CHARTER.md`), or the kill criteria fire (see `PROTECTIONS.md`).

This rule is non-negotiable. If you find yourself protecting lab time at the cost of a P0/P1 conversation, the lab has won an argument it shouldn't have. Park it.

## Quarantine rules

**One-way reference only.** The lab can read from existing workspace assets (boringsystems content, Cross-Stack ARDs, Enakl learnings, the harness pattern itself, public domain knowledge). The rest of the workspace **must have no dependency on the lab**. If something in this folder ever needs to flow into go-to-market or boringsystems, that's a deliberate cross-track decision and gets logged in the workspace decision registry — not just here.

**Do not narrate the lab to the warm graph.** Until the probe has obviously cleared or obviously failed, the lab does not exist in your conversations with named contacts. (Branson's dissent: ONE trusted person may be told, not as positioning, as a confidant. That exception is allowed; broadcasting is not.)

**Do not couple lab artifacts to existing positioning surfaces.** No lab outputs published on boringsystems unless explicitly migrated through a separate decision. The lab is downstream of everything; nothing is downstream of the lab.

## How to pick this up next time

1. Read `CHARTER.md` first — what the lab is and isn't.
2. Read `PROTECTIONS.md` second — fill in the placeholders. **No execution until they're filled.**
3. Read `QUESTIONS.md` third — pick the load-bearing question(s) and answer them, or annotate them as deliberately held open until data resolves.
4. Only then start adding execution context to this folder (e.g., `agents/`, `goals/`, `decisions/` subfolders as the work matures).
