## 5. Attentional discipline

> 5/6 advisors converged independently: the folder buys reversibility of artifacts, not reversibility of attention. The mind doesn't respect folders. The 1-hour timer doesn't respect probes. "No attachment" as a verbal commitment is not the same as actual non-attachment.

This protection cannot be encoded in a placeholder. It can only be practiced. The practice:

- **Time-box the energy, not the clock** (Naval). When the probe leaks past 9pm into the shower, the walk, the 3am wake — that is data about the probe, not failure of discipline. Note it; don't fight it.
- **Watch the moment of first signal** (Greene). The day the probe shows a flicker — a sale, a warm intro, a metric — is the day the discipline is tested. Pre-commit, in writing here, what you'll do that day:

> When first signal flickers, before celebrating, I will: `____`
>
> *(Suggested anchor: "send the accountability human a one-line message and wait 24 hours before increasing the time allocation.")*

- **Do not fall in love with the orchestration craft** (Naval). Building is more dopaminergic than catch-ups. If the probe becomes the thing you're more excited about than the 90%, the cost compounds invisibly. The accountability human's job is to call this.

This is not a placeholder to fill in writing. It is a single experiential test, run once, before any execution begins. Sit, don't open the lab, observe.
