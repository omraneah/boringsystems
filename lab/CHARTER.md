# Lab Charter

## What the lab is

A small parallel exploration probe testing one specific hypothesis:

> **In the emerging agent-to-agent attention dynamic of 2026, people positioned for agent-discoverable assets (programmatically-deliverable knowledge, lead magnets, productized information) may receive a different category of inbound than people positioned only for human-channel discovery. If the window is real, this could be a once-per-decade arbitrage. If it isn't, the cost is ~1 hour per night for some weeks.**

Concretely, the probe builds a small orchestration architecture (a meta-agent coordinating existing specialist agents toward business outcomes) and tests whether accumulated knowledge can be productized into info products that a fully-automated stack can sell.

## The five proofs being run simultaneously

1. **The architecture itself.** That the agent-swarm orchestration pattern (conductor + sub-agents + reconciliation + checkpoints) actually produces coherent business output, not just clever artifacts.

2. **The collaboration model.** That Ahmed's way of working with this whole harness — the project structure, the agent persona pattern, the Claude Code workflow — can produce real-world value at the end of the pipe, not just internal IP.

3. **The assets.** That the years of accumulated material (Enakl cases, boringsystems content, Cross-Stack ARDs, the harness pattern itself, domain knowledge across founders / GTM / marketing / sales / product / paid-acquisition / analytics / growth) can be packaged and sold programmatically — not only bit-by-bit when Ahmed is personally in the room.

4. **The economic floor.** That the system can produce a virtuous cycle of €500–€1,000 / month MRR from small info products, lead magnets, hooks, possibly LLM-augmented behind the curtain. Not €10. Not €1M. A real recurring number, modest, real.

5. **The mode.** Programmatic / automated / digital-twin-driven, not artisanal. Selling without Ahmed having to be in every transaction.

## What the lab is NOT

- **Not a pivot away from the relationship-led primary path.** The Re-Entry Doctrine still governs the 90%. The lab does not replace it.
- **Not a €10 toy demo.** The economic floor is real recurring money, just modest.
- **Not an advisory productization.** That belongs in the GTM track, not here.
- **Not coupled to boringsystems' editorial line.** Articles may emerge as a side effect; they are not the lab's purpose.
- **Not on a deadline.** No days, no quarter target. The probe runs at 10% effort until it catches or until the kill criteria fire.
- **Not a strategy.** Strategy is freedom, ownership, building a relationship-led practice. The lab is a probe, not a strategy.

## Success criterion

The lab is declared successful **if and only if** it produces, at some open horizon:

> **€500–€1,000 / month of recurring revenue** through programmatic / automated channels, with the production stack operable without Ahmed in every transaction.

This is a floor, not a target. If the probe produces signal that the agent-to-agent arbitrage is real in some other form (a warm intro that wouldn't have come otherwise, a category of inbound that wouldn't have happened, a piece of evidence the substrate is shifting), that also counts as success — but only if the underlying mechanism is named, not hand-waved.

## Integrity floor

> No duping people. Not now, not ever, not as a side effect.

Concrete edge cases (full ruling lives in `QUESTIONS.md`, item 6, until resolved):
- AI-generated content sold under Ahmed's name **with disclosure**, not without.
- Repackaging existing public boringsystems articles into a paid bundle: **OK with attribution** that they're a curated bundle of existing public work.
- Cold outreach with personalized AI-generated DMs at scale: **not OK** without explicit per-recipient relevance.
- Lead-magnet → email sequence → upsell with LLM-tuned voice: **OK with disclosure**, not OK as imitation.

The principle: **if you couldn't explain it to a respected peer without flinching, it's a dupe. Don't ship it.**

## Failure criteria

The lab is declared a failure (and parked, not deleted) when **any** of the following fires:

- The kill criteria in `PROTECTIONS.md` fire.
- 90% cadence drops measurably (warm-graph conversations from 4–5/week to 2–3/week sustained for 2+ weeks).
- The lab starts narrating itself into Ahmed's external positioning surfaces uninvited.
- The integrity floor is at risk of being crossed and the path of least resistance leads through the floor.

Parked ≠ deleted. The thinking stays in this folder for revisitation.
