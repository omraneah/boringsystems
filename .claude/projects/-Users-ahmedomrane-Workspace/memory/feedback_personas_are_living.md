Every persona file under `.claude/agents/` (Naomi, Sofia, Daniel, Hadi, Margaret, and the six strategic advisors) is a **first draft**. The voices, backstories, reference figures, embedded-community lists, operating constraints, and even the disclaimers themselves are calibrated to a snapshot of Ahmed's current thinking and may diverge from what serves him as the work matures.

**Why:** Ahmed is in an exploration phase (re-entry, France 2026) where his way of working with these personas is itself an iteration variable. A persona that landed well on the first session may need a different voice register, a different cultural anchor, a different set of reference figures, or a different operating constraint by the tenth. Treating personas as fixed creates drag — Ahmed ends up working *around* a voice that's no longer fitting him instead of rewriting it.

**How to apply:**

- When Ahmed says a persona is sounding off, anchored wrong, too forward / too restrained, importing a frame he no longer holds, or asks for a rewrite — **rewrite the file directly**. Do not negotiate with the existing draft as if it were authoritative.
- When Ahmed gives a calibration note about a persona ("Sofia is AI-forward, not pre-AI nostalgic"; "Naomi shouldn't push audience-building"; "Daniel should leave more duplication earlier"), **bake it into the file**, not just into the live conversation. The next session reloads the file, not the chat.
- The persona-vet disclaimer block in `.claude/agents/README.md` is the load-bearing acknowledgement that these are drafts. Keep it visible. Don't let the disclaimer get pruned in cleanup passes.
- When introducing a new persona, mirror the disclaimer treatment — Ahmed should be able to reject the new persona's shape after seeing it in action, the same way he can reject the existing ones.
- Iteration commits are small and isolated. One persona, one PR, one revert path. Don't bundle persona rewrites with feature work.
- Don't moralize about "consistency." Personas are tools. Tools get reshaped to fit the hand using them.
