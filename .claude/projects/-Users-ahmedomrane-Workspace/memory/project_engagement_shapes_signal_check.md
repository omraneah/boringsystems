**The instruction (Naval, 2026-04-25 strategic-board final round):** *"Let one of the four die by day 45 by weight of evidence, not by decision. The market will tell you. You don't have to choose."* Greene's parallel framing: *"Let one of the four become primary by weight of evidence, not by decision. The other three demoted to peripheral."*

**Why mid-June and not day 45.** Day 45 from 2026-04-25 = early June. But Ahmed's plan: two more weeks of session work (until ~May 9) + two-week disconnect (~May 9 → May 23) + ramp back. The signal-window starts after the disconnect ends. Realistic check: **2026-06-15** — about 7 weeks out. Enough conversations to read the pull, late enough to clear the disconnect.

**What to look for.** Across the next ~25–30 in-person conversations + any inbound from the page being forwarded:

| Shape | Pull signals |
|---|---|
| Fractional CTO / CPO | Companies past first product asking about leadership-tier presence; recurring "we need someone above the IC layer" framing |
| Complex project lead | Bigger-client transformations / migrations / cross-functional unblocking asks; "stuck multi-team" framing |
| Founder / builder sprint | Early-stage founders asking him to ship something with judgment included; the car-wash demo shape extending |
| AI-agent training | Founders or technical teams asking him to teach the harness; the live-demo-then-they-test pattern |

**Decision criterion.** *Pull is data — read it.* (Naval, again.) The shape with disproportionate inbound resonance is the one. The shape with measurably no pull at all by mid-June is the one to demote (not delete — demote: drop from active conversation, leave on the page as visible co-presence).

**Hard rule:** do NOT collapse the four during the disconnect. The point of holding them open is letting the market collapse two of them for free. Premature collapse is the failure mode the architecture is designed to prevent.

**What to NOT do at the signal check:**
- Do not edit the page in the disconnect window. The page is a signal instrument, not a thing to refine.
- Do not run another convene-board session before mid-June unless something structurally breaks. The point of the disconnect is the disconnect.
- Do not interpret silence on a shape as failure. Silence is the data; absence of pull on shape X = shape X probably isn't the one.
