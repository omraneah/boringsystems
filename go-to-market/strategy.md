## Position synthesis

> **Influence-based change leadership in complex orgs rooted in real constraints — engineering, systems, teams, operations — hands-on under half, no direct-report-heavy authority, in France, with people I'm aligned with.**

Not a standard market category. Each engagement gets co-scoped.

## Distribution architecture — three surfaces, two flows

### Shape A inbound flow → LinkedIn

- **Surface:** LinkedIn profile (headline + bio + experience).
- **Buyer:** internal recruiters / hiring managers at AI-native or transformation-shaped companies.
- **Inbound shape:** full-time mandate conversations.
- **Cadence:** profile edits driven by inbound signal. No posting cadence committed.
- **Discipline:** single-purpose surface. Don't try to make it also carry Shape B.

### Shape B inbound flow → warm network

- **Surface:** warm graph (~30-40 contacts in France, primarily Paris).
- **Buyer:** founders, peers, sponsors who refer or buy directly.
- **Inbound shape:** discovery-session-first scoping conversations. Per `memory/medium-term/Engagement-Shapes.md` § Engagement principles.
- **Cadence:** real conversations, receptive register. The graph deepens through real exchange; it does not get *processed*.
- **Referral discipline:** every converted engagement should produce 1–2 warm intros.

### Reputation currency → boringsystems

- **Surface:** boringsystems.app (case files + crystallized judgment doctrine + harness as exhibit A).
- **Buyer:** serves both flows. Recruiters doing DD on Shape A find proof of method. Warm-graph referrals doing the qualifying step on Shape B find the operating posture made legible.
- **Inbound shape:** corroborates Shape A, qualifies Shape B. Doesn't convert directly.
- **Cadence:** continue current publishing rhythm. No SEO play.

---

## Discipline

- **Don't broadcast Shape B on LinkedIn.** Shape B flows through warm intros and discovery sessions, not through profile inbound. Mixing them collapses the LinkedIn signal.
- **Don't broadcast Shape A in the warm graph.** Warm contacts forward in Shape B language ("I do X for Y companies, scoped, time-boxed"). Asking them to forward in Shape A language ("I'm looking for a role") burns the relationship.
- **Don't engineer audience-building.** boringsystems is reputation currency for both flows, not a creator-mode play.
- **Don't lock anything by date.** Drivers and filters move the frame. Distribution doesn't.
