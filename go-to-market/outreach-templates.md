# Outreach Templates

Reusable message templates for reaching into the network. Sourced from GTM-strategist sessions, validated against the relationship-primacy doctrine (`memory/medium-term/market/Re-Entry-Doctrine-Relationship-Primacy.md`).

Always tailor — never blast. The 90 seconds spent picking the right template and dropping in a specific reference doubles reply rate. Batch-sending makes a warm tie feel like a list.

---

## Warm-tie reactivation (dormant connections)

Use when reaching back to someone after a long silence. Pick by depth of the relationship, not by what's convenient.

### A — Pure reconnect, no ask

**Use for:** faded but fond. Haven't talked in 3+ years, unclear if they remember details. Earn the second message.

> Hey [Name], been way too long. How have you been? Saw something recently that reminded me of [shared context — that project / that night in [city] / the X conversation] and figured I'd say hi.

**Why it works:** nothing to decline. Reply rate is high because there's no ask. Propose a call in the second exchange, once warmth is re-established.

---

### B — Warm reconnect, soft ask

**Use for:** warm-but-shallow. Mutual respect, never deep. Default for most of the list.

> Hey [Name], been a while — how are things on your side? Would be good to actually catch up properly. If you're up for it, happy to grab 30 min on a call sometime in the next couple of weeks — no agenda, just overdue.

**Why it works:** "no agenda, just overdue" is the load-bearing phrase — removes the transactional read. "Next couple of weeks" signals not in a rush.

---

### C — Direct, names the transition

**Use for:** strong dormant ties. Worked closely, real trust. They'll be slightly insulted by vagueness.

> Hey [Name], been a while. Quick context: I'm wrapping up my run at Enakl after three years and figuring out what's next from France. You're someone whose read I'd value — would you be up for 30 min sometime in the next couple of weeks?

**Why it works:** honest, not pitchy. Names the why without selling. Only use where the relationship can carry "I'd value your read" without it feeling presumptuous.

---

## Bucketing the list

Three buckets, applied per-contact:

| Bucket | Definition | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Strong dormant ties | Worked closely, real trust earned | C |
| Warm-but-shallow | Mutual respect, never deep | B |
| Faded but fond | 3+ years silent, unclear recall | A |

## Cross-cultural notes

- **Germany:** drop "Hey man" — "Hey [Name]" reads warmer. "Man" registers as performative in cross-cultural English. Germans read directness as respect, but warm-cold mismatches register as sloppy. Tighten English — slips get noticed.

## What not to do in message #1

- Pitch any offer (even soft).
- Propose a video call as the *first* re-contact after long silence (too heavy unless tie is strong).
- Mix registers — "Hey man" + formal ask reads sloppy.
- Send the same message to >1 person without varying the specific reference.
