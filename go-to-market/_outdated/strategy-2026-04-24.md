## Buyer modes (evidence-based, from recon conversations)

Two distinct shapes, one close cannot serve both:

**Mode 1 — Operator who wants to learn to build.** (Archetype: Head of Ops who launched his own website after a 30-60 min playbook session.)
- Playbook-sharing lands.
- Referral trigger: "someone else stuck on the same mental block" (non-tech operator who thinks they can't build).
- Offers that fit: Sprint + training/playbook sessions.

**Mode 2 — Domain expert who wants an outcome.** (Archetype: Lawyer automating contract workflows.)
- Playbook-sharing does not resonate — they don't want to learn, they want someone to land the plane.
- Referral trigger: "stuck build, migration, cross-team block."
- Offers that fit: Sprint (delivered, not taught) + corporate-transformation freelance.
