**What changed in the copy.**
- Lead-in now frames the work as *decisions at the intersection of architecture, business model, and execution reality* — moves the positioning up-stack from "builds things" to "owns outcomes".
- "AI-native execution" and "AI-agent orchestration" made explicit to signal hands-on capability without landing as IC-under-manager.
- "Currently" block closes the door on pure IC roles under a lead/manager layer and opens it on Principal / Staff / Head-of mandates, fractional CTO, and complex transitions.
- Portfolio link kept — it's load-bearing proof for the AI-native claim.

**What to watch.** Whether the quality of inbound DMs and reach-outs over the next 30–60 days shifts away from pure delivery and toward fractional / advisory / transition conversations. Track in `signals.md`.

**What to not change yet.** The "open to Principal / Staff / Head of" block stays while the current role has 2 months remaining. After exit, positioning re-evaluates based on whether the 6-month freelance bet concentrates real traction.
