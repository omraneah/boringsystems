## Prep — Arnaud Porterie call · 2026-06-02 11:00 Paris (30 min)

### Anchor opener (30 sec, pre-cached)

> "12 years in tech, last 6 building functions + platforms. Last role: built the AI-assisted engineering harness for our org — 5 engineers, moved from 0% to 90%+ AI-generated new code in 14 months, 2-3× velocity baseline, 5-10× on greenfield. What I'd want to discuss with you is how I think about scaling that pattern from 5 to 50+ without losing the governance discipline that made it stick."

Set altitude in three sentences. End with a hook Arnaud can pull on.

### Three pre-cached stories — CPARL structure (Context · Problem · Action · Result · Learning)

1. **Harness rollout (Adoption story)**
   - Context: 5 engineers, multi-surface (mobile + cloud), mixed AI maturity.
   - Problem: individual AI usage, no shared governance, escalations growing, quality drifting at 50-60% adoption.
   - Action: codified 10 non-negotiable rules in Cursor governance layer + ARDs in shared repo + skills/commands as paved paths + replaced standups with agent-summaries of commits, focus standups on bottlenecks only.
   - Result: 90%+ AI-generated new code at 14 months, velocity 2-3× baseline, escalations dropped.
   - Learning: governance-before-velocity. The month spent pausing to level up CI/CD was the move that unlocked sustainable acceleration.

2. **CI/CD guardrails (Quality + Security story)**
   - Context: adoption hit 60-70%, bug count rising, escalations spiking.
   - Problem: AI-velocity-without-quality-discipline shipping silently broken code.
   - Action: two-layer guardrails — deterministic at commit hooks (no ID leaking, no untyped surfaces) + LLM-driven at PR (architectural review against ARDs) + E2E pre-prod on critical paths.
   - Result: deployments-before-3pm rule held, defects-to-prod stayed flat under 3× velocity.
   - Learning: deterministic where the rule is clear, LLM where judgment is needed. Both layers, not either-or.

3. **Scaling-the-pattern (Change-management at altitude story — hypothesis, not executed)**
   - Context: same harness, mental model for going 5 → 50+.
   - Problem: contributor-level intervention doesn't scale; influence-through-team-leads does.
   - Action: shift from "accompany the engineer" to "enable the squad through its champion" + governance layer that propagates down + feedback loop that lets squad insights propagate up.
   - Result: not yet executed at 50+ — pre-cache as your hypothesis Arnaud can stress-test.
   - Learning: at scale the surface is squads-and-functions, not contributors. Set the rules + the champions; propagation does the work.

### Mirror move

### Three traps to watch

- **Greenfield seduction.** "Build your own playbook, no other company's path" sounds liberating; without rigor it becomes scope-mutates-monthly. Probe how Arnaud bounds the scope.
- **Adoption-as-only-metric trap.** Success = "engineers actually use your product daily" is downstream of variables Ahmed doesn't fully control. Name upstream metrics that Ahmed also owns: cycle time, friction-time-saved, tooling NPS.
- **Go/Rust load.** Don't volunteer "I'd ramp Go/Rust in a few weeks via LLMs" — pull-back. Wait for him to surface stack-load expectation. If load is real, reframe as scoping question: *"What % of the role is hands-on in those vs. config/tooling/skill/agent layer?"*

### Pre-cache for likely curveballs

- *"What would you do in your first 90 days?"* — Frame as method, not roadmap: *"Diagnostic first: adoption mapping, friction-point inventory, paved-path candidates. By day 30 I'd have a sequenced 90-day plan that lands one visible win + one infrastructure layer. I don't prescribe before I diagnose."*
- *"How would you measure success?"* — Three altitudes: leading (adoption, time-to-first-commit, friction surveys), lagging (cycle time, deployment frequency, defect rate), qualitative (engineer satisfaction quarterly). Don't let "adoption" be the single metric.
- *"Walk me through a CI/CD rule prompt-to-enforcement"* — Already in Ahmed's repertoire from the Erwan call. Re-use.
