## Bet C — INVENTED — "Closing the post-readiness execution gap" / Article-4-anchored fractional advisory for ETI + scaleups

### Frame

Lead positioning: **Ahmed as a senior advisory partner for European ETI and scaleups who have decided AI matters, have a deadline (Article 4 in August, board pressure, M&A readiness, post-CTO transitions), and need a second senior judgment in the room with the actual harness to install while compliance is being satisfied.** Not "AI-readiness." Post-readiness execution. Anchor: the EU AI Act Article 4 obligation entering enforcement on August 2 2026, which forces every European enterprise to have an AI literacy program in place — and creates a discrete buying event where the buyer is "asked to procure something credible by August" with 7%-of-turnover penalties as the stick.

Buyer cohort: **French + EU mid-market ETI (250-5,000 employees) + Series A/B/C scaleups, with a CDO/CIO chair filled but light on agentic-AI execution capability.**

### Target buyer

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | French ETI Secrétaire Général, CDO, or CIO. Or French/EU scaleup CEO post-CTO-departure with Article 4 + a board AI mandate. The legally-attentive operator in a mid-sized organization. |
| Geography | Paris, Lyon, Lille — French ETI cluster cities. Berlin, Munich, Hamburg — German Mittelstand. Possibly Amsterdam / Brussels / Geneva. |
| Budget | €25-75k for a quarter-shaped engagement (diagnostic + harness install + ongoing advisory). €100-150k for a 6-month interim CTO-with-agent-handoff. OPCO-fundable for the training tail. |
| Decision speed | Discrete forcing function. Article 4 takes August 2. Buyers accelerate procurement May-July. |
| Decision basis | Regulatory teeth + sponsor confidence in the operator + concrete harness deliverable + risk-management posture |

### What makes it sellable

This is the white-space signal from Section 3 of the landscape doc. Concretely:

1. **78% of European enterprises are unprepared for Article 4 enforcement** ([Vision Compliance 2026 readiness report](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/vision-compliance-releases-2026-eu-ai-act-readiness-report-finds-78)). This is the single largest forced-buyer event in the European AI services market in 2026. Confidence: **High.**
2. **The Big-4 will quote €100-500k packages** for AI Act compliance programs ([NatLaw 2026](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/vision-compliance-releases-2026-eu-ai-act-readiness-report-finds-78)). Cheaper boutiques quote €50-100k for diagnostic-only. Solo ops can credibly land €25-75k for the diagnostic + harness install + advisory tail. Confidence: **High.**
3. **OPCO + BPI co-funding mechanisms exist** that explicitly absorb solo-expert engagements when packaged as accompagnement / formation ([Plateya 2026](https://www.plateya.fr/blog/detail/formation-ia-2026-guide-complet-financement-ai-act); [BPI Pack IA](https://blog-ia.com/aides-publiques-ia/)). 25% PME co-pay on Pack IA; 50% co-pay in Île-de-France through 2025-2026. The buyer's effective cost is half the sticker price, which compresses the procurement objection.
4. **Ahmed's prior-tenure proof maps directly to the buyer's adjacent fear** — vendor-exit, multi-tenant SaaS hardening, data infra at scale, full transformation. The buyer worried about Article 4 in August is also worried about the rest of their AI roadmap. Ahmed reads as one of the few people who can cover both.
5. **Nobody in France is leading with this combination yet.** Rémi Alvado has `accompagnement-pme` and `formation-ia-agentique` as separate pages — he hasn't fused them under the Article 4 umbrella. The Big-4 are leading with compliance and farming the harness work to internal teams. Mozza / AI Builders / Headmind are at premium price points without the regulatory anchor. **The combination of (regulatory anchor) + (harness-as-deliverable) + (single senior operator) is currently an empty quadrant.** Confidence: **High** based on competition map (Section 2.4 of landscape doc).

Three plausible paths, ranked by speed:

Could be the catalyst that turns a soft "thinking about you" into a hard "let's set up a call." 4-8 weeks lead time.

**My read on the fastest path:** path 2. Targeted outbound to 3-5 specific ex-colleagues now placed in ETI procurement-relevant chairs. Lower volume than the warm-graph, higher hit-rate per ask, more forwardable trigger. Naomi will refine the ask language.

The asset Bet A produces (Building-lane pieces, harness documentation) **stays valuable**. Drop the amplification framing; keep the artifacts. They serve Bet B and Bet C's proof layer.

The deepest possible move is to recognize the moment, name it, walk into it, and let the proof case from the first engagement reshape the next 18 months. Naomi will rightly hold the doctrine; my job is to name that the doctrine permits this and the data demands it.
