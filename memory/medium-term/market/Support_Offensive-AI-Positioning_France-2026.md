# Offensive AI Positioning - France 2026

Purpose: convert the AI disruption window into sponsor-led entry opportunities in France.

## 1) Market Shift
AI is compressing execution and increasing the value of judgment. The winning profile is not a generic manager and not a pure individual contributor. It is the builder who can redesign how work moves: constraints, decision loops, system shape, and selective implementation.

In France, the opening is uneven. Many teams want AI outcomes without having a credible execution model. That creates a wedge for short, sponsor-backed interventions that clarify the problem, reframe the system, and demonstrate velocity fast.

## 2) Positioning
Lead with:
- AI-native systems under constraint
- execution compression without loss of judgment
- sponsor-level trade-off arbitration
- visible system shifts in messy environments

Do not lead with:
- titles
- team size
- generic transformation language
- open-ended consulting

## 3) Entry Wedge
Default wedge:
- short diagnostic
- decision memo
- initial workflow or architecture reset
- bounded follow-through if sponsor quality is real

Why this works:
- lowers commitment for the buyer
- exposes sponsor quality early
- creates proof-case material fast
- preserves the option to expand or exit

## 4) Good Targets
- Funded AI-native initiatives with unclear execution shape.
- Product or engineering surfaces where AI has mandate but no operating model.
- Larger companies running internal ventures with one real sponsor.
- Small teams that need speed, not headcount theater.

## 5) Bad Targets
- AI exploration without budget or owner.
- Requests for extra hands.
- Innovation theater detached from a delivery surface.
- Diffused governance with multiple soft stakeholders.

## 6) 30-Day Offensive Loop
Week 1:
- tighten positioning language
- prepare one short proof case
- identify 10 sponsor-grade targets in France

Week 2:
- start direct outreach through warm paths and selective cold messages
- anchor every conversation on one concrete execution problem

Week 3:
- run sponsor calls
- qualify for urgency, authority, and scope
- offer a bounded wedge where fit is real

Week 4:
- convert 1-2 diagnostics or working sessions
- capture outcomes as proof material

## 7) Conversion Rules
- No free deep diagnosis before sponsor quality is clear.
- No proposals without direct line to the decision-maker.
- No full-time discussion before the problem surface is legible.
- No repeated follow-up where urgency is fake.

## 8) Leading Indicators
- Number of sponsor conversations.
- Number of concrete problems surfaced.
- Number of bounded wedges proposed.
- Number of proof cases advancing.

If these stay flat for two weeks, tighten target quality and message sharper.
