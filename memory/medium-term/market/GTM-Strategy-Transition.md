# GTM Strategy — Transition

**Status:** Authoritative — governs conversation, negotiation, and personal market presence
**Folder:** market/

---

## Core Principle (Non-Negotiable)

> You are selling a high-ticket judgment capability, not applying for a job.
> Every interaction is a B2B consultative sale — long cycles, discovery-first, sponsor-direct.

**Reconciliation — Relationship Primacy (post-July 2026):**
> GTM is relationship-first, not public-distribution-first.  
> Distribution in France flows through relationships. LinkedIn and public artifacts are support surfaces — they do not substitute for direct relationship cultivation.  
> The primary GTM motion is: warm introductions → sponsor conversations → advisory engagement.  
> Public content exists to make thinking legible to people already in conversation, not to build an audience that converts.

---

## 1. LinkedIn as Pre-Negotiation Asset

- Headshot and headline are the first credibility filter — not vanity
- The profile supports inbound from people already in the network — not cold audience growth
- Less is more: enough signal to get approached, then shape the narrative live
- Distribution layer: LinkedIn → Website → GitHub (signal → proof → artifacts)
- **Constraint:** This layer is secondary to relationship activation. Optimize it for resonance with sponsors already in conversation, not for reach.

Rule:
> Never over-explain on the profile. Intrigue pulls harder than completeness.

---

## 2. Conversation Ownership

- Lead every conversation — reduce cognitive friction, do not perform
- You enter as a consultant already inside the problem, not an outsider pitching
- Discovery before anything else:
  - Why are you talking to me?
  - What got you interested in this profile specifically?
  - What problem are you actually trying to solve?
  - What does solving it be worth?
- Ask for feedback during the call:
  - What questions should I have asked?
  - What did other candidates do that I missed?
  - Is this a clear fit from your side?

**French market social layer:**
- Brevity in messages reads as grounded. Coldness reads as reputation damage. The line between the two is one sentence of human acknowledgment.
- In France, relationship precedes transaction. Trust is signaled by tone before content is even processed.
- Even a no requires a warm door left open. A clean rejection with warmth preserves a future relationship. A clean rejection without it closes the network permanently.
- This is not warmth theater — it is a minimum register tax paid upfront so the door stays open.

Rule:
> Own the process or it owns you.

---

## 3. Negotiation Psychology

- Never anchor before understanding the ceiling and the real problem
- Understand the value of the problem before quoting anything
- Information asymmetry is always against you in logic debates — flip to emotional and psychological register
- Manufactured urgency is a tactic — slow it down, more information accumulates
- Patience = leverage. Haste = risk.
- Confidence shows in message length: the shorter, the more grounded
- Frame as WE not ME — collaboration, not confrontation
- Say no — and propose creative alternatives (performance-based, phased, hybrid structures)
- Never split the difference

---

## 4. Channel and Process Discipline

- Never negotiate through recruiters — go direct or do not go
- High-stakes conversations: video or in-person beats written
- Run conversations in territory where you are comfortable and in command
- Email is for confirmation only — never for live negotiation
- Underpromise. Overdeliver. Always.

---

## 5. Leverage Creation

- Leverage = information + timing
- Pull must exist before you anchor — they have to want you
- The more they invest in the process, the more leverage shifts toward you
- Silence is data — never fill it
- Networking rule: always give more than expected, no strings

---

## 6. Red Flags (Downgrade or Exit)

- Recruiter-only access with no path to decision-maker
- Urgency framing without a concrete problem surface
- Vague interest with no budget, mandate, or owner
- Process demands anchoring before discovery is complete
- Logic and credibility as the only conversation register

Two red flags → downgrade or exit.
