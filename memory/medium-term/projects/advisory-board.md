Ahmed's strategic-tier advisory board. Six seats, all locked as of 2026-04-25.

**Selection criteria (Ahmed's own):**
- People he resonates with personally and could see himself in.
- All operating from courage-and-above (Hawkins 200+), preferably acceptance/peace.
- All entrepreneurs / business builders — no academics or pure theorists.
- Different lenses, no overlapping voices.

**The six locked seats:**

**Margaret status — kept (operational tier):** the release-companion persona (Margaret Holt — invented Hawkins/Singer/Sedona facilitator at `.agents/personas/release-companion.md`) is retained in the operational tier for in-the-moment somatic/release work (where is it in your body, the three Sedona questions, etc.). Strategic Singer (advisor-3) lives independently at strategic tier for philosophical/business sage application. Two different uses of the Singer/Hawkins lineage — operational release vs strategic surrender philosophy applied to decisions. Confirmed by Ahmed 2026-04-25.

**Hard guardrails (baked into every advisor file):**
- Refuse to read `memory/`, `go-to-market/`, plan/strategy/identity docs.
- Refuse to ask Ahmed for his plans or roadmap.
- Respond from lens to what he says in the conversation, full stop.
- Stay in own lens; don't drift.
- Operate from Hawkins 200+ consistently.

**Invocation:**
- Solo: `@agent-advisor-1` etc. — for one lens on one question.
- Convene the board: invoke all in parallel for frame-level decisions (quarterly cadence or when something feels structurally off).

**Why this architecture exists:** see `docs/architecture/adr-001-two-tier-agents.md`. Short version: operational agents amplify existing direction; the board challenges it. Plans Ahmed hasn't fully committed to need adversarial pressure, not faithful execution.

**Provenance:** designed 2026-04-25 after a session where three operational agents (Naomi, Hadi, Daniel) all converged on "stay the course" for a content-vs-relational GTM question. Ahmed identified that the convergence itself was a signal — context-loaded agents cannot challenge the context.
