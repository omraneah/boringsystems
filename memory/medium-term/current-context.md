# Current Context — 2026 Re-Entry

The being layer is at `long-term/I-AM.md`. This file holds the operational current-state context — capability profile, geography, market specifics, transition state, entry strategy. Updates with the phase; archives when re-entry completes.

## Phase

Exiting Enakl (June 30, 2026). 3 years building TMS for a logistics startup, now transitioning to SaaS. Re-anchoring year, not conquest. Goal: re-enter on his own terms — high leverage, decision-layer work, proximity to real authority.

## Capability profile (portable, not title-based)

- Senior engineering leader (CTO-equivalent scope)
- Navigates complex, ambiguous systems rapidly
- Crisp judgment under constraint — no over-analysis
- Cross-functional: tech, product, ops, org interfaces without losing coherence
- Operational system design (how work flows, not just org charts)
- Business alignment: model, trade-offs, outcomes
- AI-native edge: uses AI to compress execution cycles, augments judgment, never outsources it

## Geographic & market context

- Based in France (Paris primary, Sfax for nature rotation)
- Primary market: France
- French market specifics:
  - Relationship-first. Small interconnected networks in Paris.
  - Tone becomes reputation before work does.
  - Warm introductions and trusted intermediaries over cold applications.
  - Every external message must close with a brief human acknowledgment — even a "no" must feel respectful.
  - Brief AND warm (never brief AND cold).

## The system being exited

Enakl shape: ambiguity + pressure, offloads responsibility upward, rewards over-functioning. Primary exit risk is re-attachment through responsibility or empathy ("quick help" traps).

## Positioning philosophy (current)

Target: **AI-native execution builder operating across systems, decisions, and transformations**.

Not title-based. Evaluate any opportunity by:
- Scope of impact (not role label)
- Decision authority (proximity to real decisions)
- System-level influence
- Whether AI is materially changing execution there

Reject if: vague AI exploration, no clear owner, execution-only framing, buried under layers without authority.
