## What goes here

Rules and facts that pass the test:

> *"Is this true today, but might be different in 6 months?"*

If yes → medium-term.

Categories:

- **Current direction snapshot** — one always-fresh file describing where Ahmed is heading right now. Updated during weekly consolidation.
- **Positioning and market doctrine** — leverage profile, market lens, re-entry doctrine, visibility, sponsor-altitude rules, GTM strategy.
- **Strategic context** — current life-phase context, work hygiene doctrine, geographic and market specifics.
- **Advisory and operating-mode rules** — strategic advisory board composition, model × effort × lane matrix, sub-agent brief discipline, persona-as-living-draft posture, parallel-agent recap, lane-change announcement.
- **Workspace conventions** — file routing, output rendering, card-fanout discipline, audit-fix isolation, batch permission patterns, post-merge workflow, planning-snapshot-before-flags, infrastructure-first.

## Auto-load

**Summary / routing index only** in `MEMORY.md`. Full content loaded on-demand when Claude is doing work that touches this layer or when Ahmed asks.

## The interpretive role

Medium-term is the layer that:

- Reads short-term experience (last 4 weeks of daily entries)
- Articulates what it means for current direction
- Flags drift candidates for long-term audit during consolidation

`current-arc.md` is the always-fresh snapshot of where Ahmed is right now. Updated during weekly consolidation. One file, evolving.

## Promotion / demotion path

- Short-term observation that has stabilized over 4 weeks → propose promotion to medium-term during consolidation.
- Medium-term claim that has held for 3+ months and become identity-shaped → propose promotion to long-term.
- Medium-term claim that no longer fits current direction → demote to short-term `_needs-consolidation/` for re-evaluation, or archive.

All movements happen during weekly consolidation, not silently.

## Conflict with live conversation

Medium-term is the most fluid tier. If live conversation contradicts a medium-term entry, default to live + flag for consolidation. Less stop-and-ask friction than long-term, more than short-term.
