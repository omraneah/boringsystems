# Pull-Mode Strategy — Three-Phase Discovery

The operational strategy for discovery conversations and engagement scoping. Three phases. Not a tactic to perform; the natural expression of [[Anchored-Stance]] under engagement conditions. Pairs with [[Engagement-Validity-Filter]] (universal failure-shape filter), [[Engagement-Shapes]] (the two surfaces), [[Drivers-and-Filters]] (the qualification layer).

---

## Position — asset-holder, not candidate

The reframe upstream of every move: **time and cognition are the asset; the engagement is an allocation decision.** Not a candidate seeking a role. Not a salesperson seeking a sale. An asset-holder evaluating allocation against the [[Drivers-and-Filters]] position synthesis.

Once this reframe is held in the body (not performed), every downstream move flows from it.

---

## Substrate dependencies (when the strategy works)

The strategy is conditional on a substrate. When the substrate degrades, the strategy collapses — the operator over-anchors on opportunity, the stance becomes performance, and the pattern reverts.

| Condition | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Runway intact** | No financial pressure to close. The walk is felt, not declared. |
| **Identity not foreclosed** | Not "I am this engagement." Sovereignty preserved. |
| **Concrete BATNA** | Tunisia low-cost option, freelance income, family resources — a real outside option the body knows. |
| **Body-level fear of repeat** | Recent contact with the cost of past misalignment keeps the strategy honest. |

Without these, "pull-mode" becomes a performance, and the operator gets caught in the same patterns the strategy was supposed to prevent. See [[Anchored-Stance]] § Conditional substrate.

## Phase 1 — Spectator + seed + read (oral, in the room)

**Precondition.** Trust is established before the room opens. No trust → don't enter. At Ahmed's altitude, the room opens because of warm intro, prior filtering, or earned context. Once in the room, the competence premise is already handled; the live question is fit.

**Moves**

1. **Spectator stance.** Let the sponsor / buyer narrate first — their world, ambition, operating model, frustrations, hidden assumptions. Don't lead with your frame. Don't pitch.
2. **Discovery objectives, executed in parallel with listening:**
   - Real sponsor (Economic Buyer in MEDDIC terms)
   - Real mandate (vs. surface description)
   - Real success criteria
   - Real timeline (rarely the date stated)
   - Other people in the buying chain
3. **Mirror their frame back.** Restate their world in your own words. Shows you got it. Subconsciously signals "this person understands" without claiming you do.
4. **Seed constraints — non-negotiables, not bargaining chips.** Place 2–4 structural constraints inside their frame, as facts not opposition:
   - Operator boundary constraints (hands-on %, main-dev role, founder-grade risk)
   - Engagement-shape constraints (cadence, time-boxing, exit gates)
   - Pre-empt the obvious traps for your archetype
5. **Don't oversell.** Overselling triggers their negotiation reflex AND signals competence-anxiety. Both collapse leverage. The constraint should land as structural information, not as an opening offer.
6. **Read response to constraints.** The constraint-response IS the diagnostic. See § three-response taxonomy below.
7. **Close without revealing commitment.** Signal *interested-not-desperate*. They should leave with the impression that you got the project, you're engaged enough to take a next step, AND you have other options.

### Calibration trap on signal "interested-not-desperate"

Neutral can read as disinterest with operators tuned for warmth. Target signal is *interested-not-lavishly-interested*, not *neutral*. Tune to operator: warm operators read warmth as competence; control-types read warmth as susceptibility.

### What phase 1 catches and what it doesn't

- **Catches:** frame-controllers (constraints get diluted or restated as fiction at exit), founder-grade-reflex operators (single teaser surfaces mid-call), aligned operators (constraints metabolized + concretized into structural projection).
- **Doesn't catch alone:** ESFP-archetype with covert-contract pattern (warmth performance bypasses constraint-response signal). Requires AI double-check on transcripts to surface.

## Phase 2 — Written sharpening + probe instrument (post-call)

Activated only if phase 1 passes (response was metabolize-with-reflex or metabolize-and-concretize, AND body-check is alive). If phase 1 produced frame-restoration → walk; no phase 2.

**Moves**

1. **You make the next move.** Don't wait. Make the next move from a position of consolidated read, not from reactivity.
2. **Constraint-sharpening in writing.** Restate phase-1 constraints harder, in writing, as structural facts. Writing creates paper trail the intermediary can forward verbatim upward.
3. **Name the structural trap to the intermediary explicitly.** If risk is concentrated on the next operator, say so out loud. The intermediary either acknowledges (re-cuts) or pretends (which a competent operator can't sustain). Forces structural reformulation.
4. **Offer a probe instrument.** Free product session, scoping session, audit-grade artifact — something that exposes your method + names your limits + helps the buyer shape the engagement that fits them. The probe IS discovery, not giveaway. Named limits make it impossible to mistake the probe for free founder-grade ownership.
5. **Two-pistes structure.** Present 2 scoped options, force choice, don't ask permission to engage. *"Either A or B; in both cases, [non-negotiable]."*
6. **Wait for all relevant sponsors to be pre-sold before bringing the harder constraint push.** Calibrate per constraint × authority: bring constraints with the highest sponsor who has authority on what you'd constrain (Miller Heiman). Not all sponsors need to be in every constraint conversation.

### What phase 2 produces

- Buyer either re-cuts to fit, or self-eliminates by going silent / dodging the probe / pushing back on the precondition.
- Sophisticated control-ENTJ surfaces here if missed at phase 1: they can't tolerate the structural reformulation forced by the written sharpening.
- Intermediary's behavior tests fit-selector vs. arbitrager: a fit-selector absorbs and routes; an arbitrager pushes back on the probe.

## Phase 3 — Win-win negotiation with BATNA-anchored walk (inside the engagement scope)

Activated only after phase 2 produces a buyer who has metabolized the structural reformulation. By this point the operator is hooked; you have the information; the conversation has become real negotiation.

**Moves**

1. **Win-win frame** (Fisher/Ury): interests not positions, mutual-gain options, objective criteria. The buyer is now invested; collaborative framing closes faster than adversarial.
2. **BATNA never softens.** Sunk-cost pressure makes them push harder on constraints. The walk-readiness that produced phase 2 success must remain unchanged in phase 3. Voss is right on this: the relationship is still asymmetric; "fair" can still be manipulation.
3. **Negotiate terms, not rate.** Scope, time, cognitive load, authority, exit gates. Money is the last variable to land — see [[Engagement-Shapes]] § Money as shape-health signal.
4. **The close is the buyer asking to buy.** In high-ticket B2B, sellers don't close; buyers do. *"J'espère qu'on se voit dans pas très très longtemps" / "On en reparle mercredi" / similar* = buyer-asking move. Don't push past it; receive it.

### Phase-3 limit

Even in win-win mode, the strategy commits to walking if constraints get diluted. Doctrine: **BATNA never softens.** If phase-3 negotiation requires giving up a phase-1 non-negotiable, the gate was wrong upstream — walk, don't accommodate.

## The three-response taxonomy (operator types revealed by phase 1)

| Response shape | Operator type | Doctrine outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame restoration via fiction or soft denial + re-assertion** | Frame-controller (sophisticated control-ENTJ in low-governance) | Walk. No phase 2. |
| **Metabolization + one founder-grade reflex** (e.g. intéressement teaser, scope creep request) | Honest constraint-metabolizer with founder-grade default | Defuse structurally in phase 1, watch in phase 2. If reflex repeats → walk. |
| **Metabolization + structural concretization** (names the scope inside the frame) | Aligned operator (governance present, developed F, peer constraints already internalized) | Continue. Phase 2 sharpens the remaining structural questions. |

Data points: Whitebird (Benoit, sophisticated ENTJ) → restoration → walk. Exeko (Maxime) → reflex (intéressement) → defuse + Louis-Angelina watch. Generali (Yousra) → concretization → Linda call. Vibe.co → metabolization → Arnaud Porterie call. All four 2026-05-26 → 2026-05-28.

## Operator-type filter (what the strategy detects, what it doesn't)

### ENTJ — caught by nature

ENTJs don't perform empathy. Their self-centeredness, control frequency, and negotiating-with-non-negotiables IS the diagnostic. **Sophisticated ENTJs are also caught**, because the wiring is the signal — sophistication doesn't suppress the Te-dominance. Catch happens at phase 1 if discipline holds; phase 2 written-sharpening confirms. Failure mode is operator-side (letting them reframe), not strategy-side.

Data points: Whitebird (Benoit), current company founder — both sophisticated ENTJ, both detectable at intake when discipline held.

### ESFP — untested, most risky

ESFPs perform warmth. Their passive-aggressive, covert-contract pattern bypasses the constraint-response diagnostic. The signal is hidden in tone, body language, and emotional sub-text — not in explicit response to seeded constraints. **AI double-check on transcripts is the required detection instrument.** Ahmed's gut alone is insufficient at intake.

Hard rule: **ESFP is a lifetime hard no** (per [[Engagement-Validity-Filter]] § low-governance relational fit). Detection precedes acceptance.

### N-over-S structural filter

**Never work directly under an S-type sponsor.** The N upstream is the structural shield against S-type tactical-vs-strategic mismatch. If the direct sponsor reads tactical and Ahmed reads strategic, the engagement compounds asymmetry: Ahmed sees structure they don't, they see process he doesn't honor, and the value Ahmed brings is unintelligible to them. This is a structural filter, not a preference.

### Smarter-than-direct-sponsor as walk signal

If Ahmed consistently sees structure / patterns / risks the direct sponsor doesn't see — and the sponsor doesn't grow into the gap within the first phase-2 cycle — walk. The strategic-tactical asymmetry collapses the engagement value from both sides.

## Money — shape-health signal, not primary variable

Detailed treatment in [[Engagement-Shapes]] § Money as shape-health signal. Operative summary:

- Money is the **last** variable in negotiation, not the first.
- Money is a **signal** of engagement-shape health, not the leverage being optimized.
- Negotiate **terms** (scope, time, cognitive load, authority, exit gates) — not rate.
- If shape requires more engagement than envelope allows → reduce scope or walk; don't bargain rate.
- Pricing-anchor instrument ("around X, depends on mandate") works with aligned operators; invites downward negotiation with frame-controllers. Calibrated to operator.

## High-ticket B2B sales lineage (where the strategy comes from)

The strategy is not invented. It maps to real frameworks from the high-ticket B2B sales literature. Named here so future-Ahmed can read the source material when sharpening is needed.

| Framework | Source | What it provides |
|---|---|---|
| **SPIN Selling** | Neil Rackham (IBM/Xerox research) | Top performers in large sales spend ~60% in Investigating. Talk less, ask deeper. The buyer tells you the deal. → Phase 1 spectator + discovery. |
| **Challenger Sale** | Dixon/Adamson (CEB study of 6000 reps) | Challengers beat Relationship-Builders 4:1 in complex B2B. Teach + tailor + take control. → Constraint-seeding as teaching. |
| **Sandler Selling System** | David Sandler | Pressure-free posture is highest-converting. "You don't need this deal" psychology. Up-Front Contract. → Freedom-as-BATNA + phase-2 written sharpening. |
| **MEDDIC / MEDDPICC** | Dick Dunkel (PTC) | Map Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Champion before close. → Phase 1 discovery objectives. |
| **Strategic Selling** | Miller Heiman | Engage all 4 buying influencers (Economic, User, Technical, Coach). Red Flags = structural risks. → Wait until all sponsors pre-sold before constraint push. |
| **Getting to Yes** | Fisher & Ury | BATNA strength = negotiating power. Interests not positions. Mutual-gain options. → Phase 3 win-win + BATNA anchor. |
| **Never Split the Difference** | Chris Voss | Tactical empathy, mirroring, late no, calibrated questions. "Fair" is often manipulation. → Phase 1 mirroring + phase 2 named-trap-to-intermediary. |

The strategy reads as **Sandler + Voss-leaning, with SPIN discovery and MEDDIC mapping inside.** Less Rackham (too cooperative), less Raskin (too narrative). Voss + Sandler match Ahmed's wiring: seller-sovereignty, naming truth, walking as power.

## Game-theory naming

- **Sequential Bayesian game with credible outside option.** Buyer's type is hidden; constraint-seeding extracts type-revealing moves while you withhold interest. BATNA = the credible outside option.
- **Screening mechanism** (Spence; Stiglitz-Rothschild). Constraints function as screening contracts producing a separating equilibrium between fit and non-fit operators.
- **Reversed commitment problem** (Schelling, inverted). Most negotiators credibly commit to a position; you credibly commit to walking. Walking is the harder commitment to fake and the more powerful one when held.
- **Information asymmetry preservation.** Withholding interest at call-end keeps the asymmetry on your side while seeded constraints extract type info from theirs.
