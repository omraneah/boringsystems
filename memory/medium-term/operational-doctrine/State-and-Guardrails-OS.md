# State and Guardrails OS

This document governs state regulation, boundary discipline, and correction loops. The being layer is at `long-term/I-AM.md`; this file holds the daily/weekly operational discipline that protects the being from drift.

## 1) Core laws
- Clarity over control. When blurred, slow down until intent is clean.
- Presence is power. Calm tone and deliberate pace regulate systems.
- Boundaries equal freedom. Each "no" expands energy bandwidth.
- Connection over proof. Build through resonance, not persuasion.
- Energy is the true budget. Guard it like capital.

---

## 2) State discipline

### Operating philosophy (old -> new)
- Ambition: pressure -> clarity
- Energy: speed -> presence
- Reaction: emotional -> neutral
- Control: micromanage -> energy boundaries
- Growth: output -> state maintenance

### Inner regulation loop
- Morning: sunlight + breath (4-7-8 x 5) before screens.
- Mid-day: one deep breath before context switch.
- Evening: walk/stretch + 3-line journal (drained / gave / released).

### Weekly maintenance
- One daylight walk without phone.
- One creative outlet.
- One recovery ritual.
- One connection outside work.

---

## 3) Boundary protocol

### Boundary zones
- Time: async first, protect deep-focus blocks.
- Emotional: do not fix chaotic people; state facts once then detach.
- Information: document decisions, clarity over persuasion.
- Availability: quiet hours are sacred; no weekend or late replies.
- Energy: stop when irritation rises; walk, breathe, re-enter.

### Communication template
"Here is the current status. Here is what is pending. Here is my next step."

---

## 4) Guardrails and correction

### Primary blind spots -> countermeasures
- Control reflex -> ship at 95 percent, step back 24h before refining.
- Loyalty as debt -> return ownership: "Whose decision is this?"
- Fixer optics -> require authority and budget or drop.
- Scarcity bias -> apply filters; scarcity never pays back.
- Validation hunger -> replace approval metric with calmness metric.
- Over-optimization -> stop at clarity.
- Premature confrontation -> document quietly, act when option exists.
- Energy leak -> stillness protocol (breathe x5, walk, journal 3 lines).

### Stance under dysfunction
- Dysfunction is normal in stressed systems.
- Exposure must be bounded.
- Vigilance is instrumental, not emotional.
- Watch-out: vigilance drifting into permanent distance.

### Correction sequence (reset protocol)
1) Stop movement - walk 5 minutes or breathe 4-7-8.
2) Label pattern - say it out loud.
3) Document reality - one sentence fact.
4) Decide scope - what is mine vs not mine.
5) Act or exit - ship, delegate, or pause.
6) Reflect after 48h - "What belief did I protect?"

---

## 5) Communication under pressure

Tone rule: calm over speed.

If manipulation appears:
- Mirror once for clarity.
- If circular, freeze the loop, document, move async.
- Never fill silence.

---

## 6) Emotional regulation kit

- Agitation: 20-min walk or cold water; write 3 facts + 1 decision.
- Fatigue: power nap or deep breath; write "What can wait?"
- Overwhelm: body scan or box breathing; focus on one sensory object.
- Irritation: 10 push-ups or shake out arms; repeat "I am not the system."
