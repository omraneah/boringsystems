# Engagement Validity Filter

The shape of an engagement that drains while looking exciting. Named so it can be detected early.

## The archetype

> **A high-payoff engagement on ONE dimension that silently costs many others — body, place, network, health, assets, positioning.**

The pattern: the work is interesting, the curiosity is real, the surface signals look fine. Underneath, multiple non-negotiable dimensions degrade simultaneously:

- **Body:** movement, yoga, sleep, body-as-compass — drifting.
- **Place:** geographic anchoring violated; commute or relocation breaking daily texture.
- **Connectedness:** chosen graph going silent; relationships maintained only with the exception that survives anything.
- **Health:** indicators sliding; sleep, energy, or biomarkers.
- **Assets:** financial / intellectual / network / reputation not compounding; knowledge captive to the system.
- **Positioning:** the engagement-shape reads as a tax in target markets; reputation is not compounding the way years of effort should produce.

**The signal that ONE dimension is being satisfied at the cost of MANY others — that's the alarm.**

## How to use this filter

Apply at three altitudes:

1. **Path-decision altitude.** When evaluating any engagement (employment, freelance, fractional, advisory, own-thing), check: which dimensions does this pay across? If the answer is "primarily curiosity" or "primarily income" or "primarily status" or "primarily anything-singular" — the path is at risk of replaying the failure shape.
2. **In-engagement altitude.** When already in a path, check (monthly cadence is enough): which dimensions are being silently traded off? If body, place, connectedness, family, sleep, or own-things-accumulation are degrading — the engagement is drifting toward the failure shape, even if the work itself remains interesting.
3. **Decision-pressure altitude.** When pressure to commit fast appears (recruiter inbound, founder pitch, deadline framing), the failure shape most often slips in through the dimension being optimized in the moment. The fix is not "decide faster" but "check across all dimensions before deciding."

## The opposite shape — what alignment looks like

A healthy engagement pays across multiple dimensions simultaneously:

- **Body intact.** Movement happening. Energy stable. Sleep good.
- **Place intact.** Geographic anchoring honored. No commute that breaks the day-to-day texture.
- **Family / relationship intact.** Wife seen daily. Family time real. (Future: child + family time honored.)
- **Connectedness intact.** Real conversations with the chosen graph happening regularly.
- **Health intact.** Stable or improving baseline.
- **Assets compounding.** Financial, intellectual, network, reputation — at least 2–3 of these moving forward.
- **Positioning compounding.** Each engagement adds to the visible portable-leverage profile, doesn't subtract.
- **Curiosity satisfied.** Interesting work happening. Heat is real.
- **Income sufficient.** Money flowing at a level that doesn't create haste.

When **most or all** of these are simultaneously alive, the engagement is well-aligned. When **only one or two** are satisfied, the failure shape is forming.

## The two-week rule

If the failure shape is detected at in-engagement altitude, the response is:

1. Name what's being silently traded off (specific dimension).
2. Check whether the trade-off is a brief calibration period (acceptable, time-boxed) or a structural feature of the engagement (not acceptable).
3. If structural: plan exit. Two weeks of structural mis-alignment is enough to confirm; do not wait longer hoping it will shift.
