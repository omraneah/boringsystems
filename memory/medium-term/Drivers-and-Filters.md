# Drivers and Filters — Next Step

The operational center for the next engagement. Codified after the 2026-05-20 session named, with grief attached and the body confirming, the load-bearing drivers and filters that govern what comes next.

This file sits below the I-AM (being-layer, life-level) and applies its felt-shape to the next step. The drivers are what pulls in this period; the filters are what disqualifies. Together they define a narrow middle — the **position synthesis** at the end.

---

## Drivers (what pulls)

What the body confirms as alive, aligned, worth moving toward.

### 1. Serve

Respond to what life arranges, when alignment and clean relationship exist. **Felt-test, not category.** The three conditions of a valid serve:

1. The encounter arrives. Not engineered.
2. The relationship is not broken or misaligned at the root.
3. Not attached to my own want, fear, or proving.

Singer-echo. The for-others work is a valid path when chosen freely from anchored kindness — see [[Path-Doctrine]] § Singer-echo and [[Surrender-Doctrine]].

### 2. Curiosity — change in complex organizations

Where the interest is real: the psychology, the incentives, the influence patterns, the system constraints, the change that has to move through them. Not "interesting tech." The interesting object is the organization in motion.

### 3. Alignment with people

Same incentives. Same sense of what matters. Not killing-oneself culture. Working with people whose register matches; whose "good" matches mine.

### 4. Freedom

Each engagement should free me further, not capture me. Sovereignty over identity and time stays intact. Felt-test: does this engagement *open* the day or *close* it.

### 5. Calm aliveness

Life is okay as it is. The engagement *adds*, it doesn't *replace* what's already alive. Engagements that arrive from completion attract well; engagements that arrive from compensation attract badly.

### 6. Geographic rootedness — France, Paris

Medium-term application of the being-level "I am rooted" (see [[I-AM]]). Build around me. Serving people physically around me > serving the other end of the planet. Not absolute (international remote engagements possible if alignment is exceptional) — but a strong default, not a preference.

### 7. Rootedness in reality and its constraints

Systems. Engineering. Teams. Operations. Move *through* constraints, don't break them. Engineering is one possible expression, not the driver itself. The driver is the engagement with real, constrained, operating reality — not abstraction.

### 8. Serendipity

Trust what arrives through closed doors. Don't engineer the path; receive it. Per [[I-AM]] Philosophy: *the path shows itself as it's walked.*

## Filters (what disqualifies)

Out-conditions. If a filter fires, the engagement is out — regardless of how compelling the rest looks.

| Filter | Out-condition |
|---|---|
| **Hands-on > 50 %** | Engagement where coding / direct execution exceeds half the time. Out. |
| **Co-founder risk-shape** | Founder-grade incentives on a non-founder engagement. Out. See contained-risk principle in [[Engagement-Shapes]]. |
| **Pure-executor / kernel-IC** | Roles that primarily live inside a terminal or IDE. Out. |
| **Hustle / prove-something framing** | Anything where the implicit job is to prove worth, demonstrate hustle, perform busyness. Out. |
| **Misaligned incentives / kill-yourself culture** | Where the team's incentives or operating tempo demand self-erasure. Out. |
| **International-disconnected from physical world** | Engagements that sever the daily-physical-presence loop. Strong preference, not absolute — but a heavy out-default. |
| **Analytics-as-tail-of-business / data-pleasing-the-business** | Explicit door close. The data-org-serving-the-business shape is named and refused. |
| **Team-leadership-as-main-mandate** | This period. Influence over authority. Out — even when the offer is well-shaped. |
| **Engineering-as-only-surface** | Laptop + screen only. Out. The work must touch people, systems, decisions — not just the editor. |
| **Founder-level risk ownership** (Shape B specific) | Carrying business outcome, market fit, runway risk on a fractional engagement. Contained risk only. Out. |

The two-week rule applies (see [[Engagement-Validity-Filter]] § two-week rule): if a filter starts firing inside an engagement, exit planning begins.

## Position synthesis (the narrow middle)

What the drivers and filters define together:

> **Influence-based change leadership in complex orgs rooted in real constraints — engineering, systems, teams, operations — hands-on under half, no direct-report-heavy authority, in France, with people I'm aligned with.**

Not a standard market category. Each engagement gets co-scoped. See [[Engagement-Shapes]] for the two surface shapes (A: mandate inside a company; B: fractional / freelance via warm network).

## How to use this file

- **At decision-time** for any engagement (inbound, intro, opportunity): read the eight drivers, then the ten filters. The right shape lights up the drivers AND clears the filters.
- **Inside an engagement** (monthly): re-read the filters. If one starts firing silently, name what's being traded off, give it two weeks, exit if structural.
- **When framing a conversation** (DM, warm intro reply, scoping call): use the position synthesis as the one-sentence frame.
