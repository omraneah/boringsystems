When briefing strategic-tier advisors via convene-board (or any solo invocation), never amplify a single sentence from a long-arc identity document into a "live current variable." Grep for fresh signal in conversation-state files (`go-to-market/`, recent transition notes, recent decisions) before composing the brief, check timestamps, and ask Ahmed before importing personal/sensitive details from his identity substrate.

**Why:** 2026-04-25 session — sourced one month-old sentence about fertility from `memory/long-term/inner-game/Meta-Identity-Constitution.md` and converted "is the live variable" → "live considerations right now." Five of six advisors anchored on it heavily. When Ahmed pushed back ("how is that related?"), ran a *second* convene that re-anchored on the same questionable signal instead of stopping to verify. Compounded a brief-craft error into a session-derailing rabbit hole.

**How to apply:**
1. Before composing a board brief, grep live-state files for what Ahmed has named in the last 1–2 weeks. Use that as the substrate, not the identity-layer documents.
2. Keep the brief to material from the conversation itself plus surface-level current state. Identity docs are interpretive — they were written for a different purpose than briefing third parties.
3. When Ahmed pushes back on any framing, **stop and verify** before running another convene. Don't double down. The first push-back is the calibration signal.
4. Never import personal, relational, health, or family details from identity docs without explicit confirmation they are still live at the time of the brief.
5. Advisors are context-naive by design — anything in the brief becomes load-bearing. Asymmetry of weight: small wording in the brief = large wording in the synthesis.

**Provenance:** 2026-04-25 session. Cost: derailed a multi-hour conversation, wasted advisor calls, generated a synthesis Ahmed had to manually correct.
