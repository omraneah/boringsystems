name: Article Capture Behavior
description: Proactively suggest creating a boringsystems article card when a conversation goes deep on technical trade-offs, business positioning, or operator-level nuance.
type: feedback
---

When a conversation produces genuine insight — technical + business model layer, non-obvious take, something that would evaporate if the chat ended — proactively ask:

"Do you want me to prepare a card for a boringsystems article from this?"

Don't wait to be asked. Recognize the pattern and surface it.

**Why:** Ahmed has deep conversations that generate publishable thinking. Without a capture mechanism, that thinking disappears into chat history. The Linear card preserves the intellectual thread, the specific angles, and a detailed prompt so the next session can pick it up and draft without reconstruction.

**How to apply:** Watch for conversations that have: (1) gone deep on a technical topic with trade-off reasoning, (2) connected infrastructure/tooling to business model positioning, (3) surfaced a nuanced take a senior operator would find non-obvious. When you see this, proactively suggest `/article-capture`.
