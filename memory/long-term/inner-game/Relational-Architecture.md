# Relational Architecture — Depth Expansion

Companion to `../I-AM.md` § *Relational architecture — network as net worth*. The high-level principles live there; the operational depth lives here.

This file is **doctrine, not plan**. Updates when a layer's definition is revealed to be wrong, when an exit protocol fails to fit a real case, or when a new dimension of relational health surfaces. Otherwise: leave alone.

---

## The three diversity layers

A healthy relational architecture has **all three layers active simultaneously**, not any one dominant. Monoculture in any direction is fragility.

### Layer 1 — Small circle of deep trust

The 3–7 people who get the unedited me. Family (wife, future child, parents, siblings), the closest friendships, possibly one or two long-running collaborators over time.

- **Test:** can they say something that destabilizes me, and does the relationship not break?
- **Cadence:** doesn't matter. They're load-bearing whether contacted weekly or monthly.
- **Mode:** truth-first. Niceness is failure here. Kindness is the floor.
- **Investment:** maximum. These are non-substitutable.

### Layer 2 — Wider network of mutual value-exchange

The 30–200 people I've worked with, exchanged value with, where the relationship is real but not deep. Past colleagues, advisors, founders I've helped, people who've helped me.

- **Test:** if I asked for a favor, would they help (or refer)? If they asked, would I?
- **Cadence:** quarterly to annually for most; situational for active conversations.
- **Mode:** giver-default, mutual-eventually. Reciprocity over time, not per-interaction.
- **Investment:** episodic but real. A real conversation every quarter beats ten "still alive?" pings.

### Layer 3 — Connector routing

The few people whose role in my life is to route serendipity. They know lots of people, see patterns, make introductions. Their value isn't in our 1:1 relationship — it's in what flows through them.

- **Test:** does an honest update to them produce surprise-and-relevant signal back?
- **Cadence:** episodic. Touch when there's real signal to share or ask about.
- **Mode:** information-rich, low-extraction. Their currency is patterns; mine is being interesting to route.
- **Investment:** asymmetric (they often give more than I do, in routing terms; I balance with being a high-quality node for them to route).

**Monoculture is fragility:**

- All Layer 1 (insular) → I become invisible to the wider world; opportunities don't surface; serendipity doesn't reach.
- All Layer 2 (no inner circle) → no one to be undefended with; identity erodes through low-grade performance.
- All Layer 3 (no depth) → exhaustion; presence-without-substance; no real friends.

The architecture needs all three breathing simultaneously.

## Anti-fragility specifics

A relationship is **anti-fragile** when:

- **Truth survives it.** The hard conversation can happen and the relationship comes out stronger.
- **Asymmetry corrects.** When one side gives more for a season, the other notices and rebalances eventually — without anyone tracking.
- **Time apart doesn't kill it.** Six months of silence is fine; you pick up where you left off.
- **The other person is anchored.** Their identity isn't dependent on this relationship; they have their own ground.
- **Value flows in multiple registers.** Not just utility. Care, friendship, intellectual stimulation, presence — multiple channels.

A relationship is **fragile** when:

- **Truth threatens it.** Honest disagreement creates fear of rupture.
- **Asymmetry calcifies.** One side is always giving / always taking, indefinitely.
- **Maintenance is required.** Constant contact / constant signaling / constant performance to keep it alive.
- **The other person needs it.** They are dependent on this relationship to feel okay.
- **Value flows in one register only.** Pure utility, pure validation, pure emotional support — single-channel relationships are usually transactional in disguise.

The lens is felt, not deduced. When the felt-signal says "this won't survive truth," it usually doesn't. Trust the signal.

## Exit protocols

When a relationship needs to end, it ends. Three categories:

### Type A — Identity-dependent (exit cleanly)

- They require me to be a former version of myself.
- The relationship was real then; the version I have to be for it isn't real now.
- **Exit:** warm acknowledgment, no drama, no elaborate explanation. The other person may or may not understand. That's not the metric. Don't perform niceness. Don't perform regret either. Just exit.

### Type B — Parallel non-interfering (retain selectively)

- Compatible at low contact; not compatible at high contact.
- **Redesign the surface:** less contact, less depth, less signal-sharing.
- They stay in Layer 2 or 3 by being held there deliberately.

### Type T — Toxic (see the end early)

- Felt-signal of misalignment, extraction, or destabilization that's not anti-fragile.
- **Exit BEFORE the relationship has crystallized publicly.** The earlier the exit, the cleaner it is for everyone.
- French market warmth floor: even toxic exits get one warm sentence. Reputation in small graphs is downstream of *how* you exit, not just whether.

### The two-conversation rule

If a relationship needs to end, two clear conversations is the maximum:

1. **The first names the misalignment / asymmetry / felt-issue.** Gives the other side a chance to correct.
2. **The second is the exit, if conversation 1 didn't shift things.**

Three+ conversations on the same misalignment = drift. Either I'm not exiting when I should, or the relationship is anti-fragile and I'm reading it wrong. Stop and check.

## Weekly check (relational hygiene)

Mirror of `../../medium-term/operational-doctrine/Work-Hygiene-Doctrine.md` § *Weekly check*, applied to relationships:

1. **Did this week feed Layer 1?** Time with wife, family, closest people.
2. **Did this week activate Layer 2?** At least one real exchange (call, meeting, useful message) with someone in the wider network.
3. **Did Layer 3 see anything from me?** Even a one-line "saw this, thought of you" to a connector counts.
4. **Did any relationship drift toward extraction?** Either direction. Notice + correct early.
5. **Did I exit anything that should have been exited last week?** Two consecutive weeks of "should have ended this" = exit this week.

Two consecutive weeks of drift on any of the five → correct immediately.

## Application across domains

### In work / engagements

- The Enakl-derailment archetype's *connectedness* dimension is downstream of this architecture. A path that violates connectedness fails the path-doctrine necessary conditions.
- Sponsor relationships are Layer-2-or-deeper; if they don't graduate beyond pure-utility, that's a signal.
- A client who treats me as execution-capacity violates the no-extraction principle. Exit.
- A client who shows real care about the work AND the relationship is Layer-2-eventual.

### In family

- Layer 1 by structural position. The non-negotiable is *presence* — not effort, not performance — presence.
- Asymmetry is allowed across long timescales (I give more in a season; she gives more in another). It is not allowed across years.
- Future child enters Layer 1 by structural position. That role asks for the most reliable version of the day-to-day texture.

### In friendships

- Layers 1 and 2 both apply, depending on the friend.
- A friendship that has stopped feeling alive is exiting on its own; honor the exit by not forcing maintenance.
- New friendships earn their way into layers — Layer 3 first, then Layer 2 over time, then Layer 1 only after years and felt-trust.

### In advisory / for-others work

- Per `Path-Doctrine.md`: "for-others" is a valid path when the relational architecture holds.
- The Singer-echo applies: working for clients / students / customers IS the path, when the relational architecture is honored.
- Watch for asymmetric extraction in either direction. A client who gives nothing back beyond the contract → fragile, not anti-fragile.

### In the warm-graph reactivation (current)

- Reactivation conversations are Layer-2 maintenance. Goal isn't transaction — it's keeping the layer breathing.
- "Game" framing (testing messaging, watching what resonates) is fine for the reactivation work itself, *not* for the relationships. The people are not the game.
- A reactivation that turns extractive (each side angling for what the other can produce) violates the architecture and produces no real value.
