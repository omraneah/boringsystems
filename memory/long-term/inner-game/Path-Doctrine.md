# Path Doctrine — The 2–3 Year Sprint to the North Star

**Status:** doctrine, not plan, not commitment. Holds the shape-rules and necessary conditions for the sprint that builds and consolidates the path to the North Star. **Does NOT commit a specific shape** — that emerges as life shows up (per the Singer-archetype in `../I-AM.md`).

**Window:** approximately 2027 → 2029 (ages 37–39). Begins after the 2026 re-stabilization phase.

---

## The frame

The 2–3 year sprint sits between the medium-term re-stabilization phase and the multi-decade North Star. It's the window where compounding starts to show — where the being-attractor produces meaningful convergences in the dimensions named in `../I-AM.md` § *What being attracts*.

The sprint is done **in a fun, loving, North-Star-aligned way.** Not in striving / hustle / pressure register. The discipline is *holding the day-to-day texture intact while the shape compounds.*

---

## Necessary conditions (all must hold; non-negotiable)

A valid path-shape during the sprint must satisfy ALL of the following. If any fails, the path is wrong, regardless of how compelling other dimensions look.

1. **Day-to-day texture intact.** Body, sleep, family, presence, kindness, no haste — every day, repeatedly. The *Day-to-day texture* section of `../I-AM.md` is the felt-test. If a path violates it more than briefly, the path is wrong.
   - **Sub-note on money:** Salary reduction is explicitly acceptable when the trade is ease of mind, no toxicity, and intact freedom. The wealth condition (from `../I-AM.md`) is about felt-state, not floor amount. A path that pays less but holds the texture intact is **not a downgrade — it is correctly calibrated.** This is a durable posture, not a phase-bound concession; it overrides any "money is not the focus right now" framing in shorter-horizon docs.
2. **Aligned with the I AM.** Sovereign, calm, present, content, loving, kind-not-nice, **relational**, courageous-not-striving, curious, free, wealthy, creator. If the path requires becoming any anti-being (scarce, striving, status-performing, validation-seeking, captured, etc.) — no.
3. **Relational architecture honored.** No extraction in either direction. Anti-fragility is the lens. Diverse layers (Layer 1 deep trust + Layer 2 mutual exchange + Layer 3 connector routing) all active. See `Relational-Architecture.md` for the full filter. **A path that violates the relational architecture is wrong, even if all other conditions hold.**
4. **Enough time and space for own things to accumulate.** The sprint isn't pure for-others. Own creations — writing, experiments, tools, communities, whatever shape emerges — need room to pile up, accumulate, concretize on their own terms. Without that room, the sovereign-creator dimension dies.

If all four hold simultaneously: it's a candidate path. If any fails: it's wrong, regardless of other strengths.

---

## One valid blueprint (NOT the only one)

An operating frame that satisfies the necessary conditions:

- **~80% of time** spent in service to others — someone else's mission, company, project, students, clients, whatever. This is where most of the income comes from, where most of the daily exposure lives, where most of the learning compounds.
- **~20% of time** on own things — creations, writing, experiments, tools, communities — that pile up and start to compound on their own terms.

**The 80/20 ratio isn't fixed.** Could be 50/50. Could be 90/10. Could be seasonal (heavy for-others one quarter, heavier own-things the next). The split serves the necessary conditions; it's not enforced for its own sake.

Other valid blueprints: full-time employment in a deeply aligned organization with own-things compounded on the side; multi-client fractional with own-things in the gaps; sabbatical-shaped own-things bursts between for-others sprints; etc. **Shape is open. Conditions are not.**

### Altitude of the for-others work

Transformation / accompaniment altitude, not execution altitude. Influence-based change leadership in complex orgs rooted in real constraints — engineering, systems, teams, operations. Technical depth is what makes the change credible; it is not the deliverable.

The container shape stays open; the altitude does not. **The medium-term expression of this — the two engagement shapes (A, A.1, B) and the filters that disqualify wrong-shape opportunities — lives in [[Drivers-and-Filters]] and [[Engagement-Shapes]].**

## The Singer-echo

Working for others is **NOT** a betrayal of the sovereign-creator path. Singer is the model:

- **Yoga:** students.
- **Construction:** clients.
- **Medical Manager** (built to ~$4B): customers.

Almost never "build my own pure thing in isolation." Singer's life was for-others-shaped throughout — and yet he was the most sovereign creator of his generation. **The sovereignty was in the *posture* (surrender, presence, freedom from grasping), not in the percentage of time spent on own-things.**

For-others CAN BE the path, when chosen freely from anchored kindness, when aligned with the necessary conditions above. This dissolves a common false conflict that affects technical operators of his profile: *"if I take a job, I'm betraying my sovereignty."* False. The job (or the contract, or the mandate) is fine if the conditions hold.

## Anti-patterns (not this)

### The single-dimension trap

The shape that drains while looking exciting:

> **A high-payoff engagement on ONE dimension that silently costs many others — body, place, network, health, assets, positioning.**

A path that satisfies *only* deep curiosity (e.g., "interesting engineering," "emerging-market exposure") while costing the rest is misaligned, regardless of how interesting the curiosity is. **The signal that ONE dimension is being satisfied at the cost of MANY others — that's the alarm.**

The sprint must pay across multiple dimensions simultaneously. Not just curiosity. Not just income. Not just status. **Body + family + place + network + health + own-creation accumulation + financial stability + curiosity. Multiple at once.** Where any of these is being silently traded off, the path is in drift.

→ Engagement Validity Filter (operational test) → `../../medium-term/operational-doctrine/Engagement-Validity-Filter.md`

### Team-leadership-as-main-mandate (this period)

A path whose primary mandate is to *lead a team* (manage direct reports, carry headcount, run an org function) fails the period-specific filter. The work this period is **influence over authority** — change leadership through judgment, presence, and the system layer, not through reporting lines. A team-lead-shaped role is wrong regardless of altitude or compensation while this period holds.

(This is a *this-period* anti-pattern, not a life-level one. It may change in a later sprint chapter. Currently load-bearing.)

## Compounding mechanics

The sprint compounds when:

- **Daily texture is consistent** (not perfect, but consistent enough that body-state is stable).
- **The for-others work is in real, non-extractive systems** (not extractive both ways — doesn't extract from the operator, doesn't put the operator into extracting others).
- **Own-things have a home** — somewhere they accumulate visibly. Could be the workspace. Could be `boringsystems.app`. Could be elsewhere that emerges. The point is they're not lost as private files.
- **Relationships are deepening** — small circle of trust, wider circle of mutual value-exchange, sponsor proximity in for-others work.

When any of these breaks, compounding stalls. When all four hold simultaneously, things start showing up that no single move could have produced.

## What "successful sprint" looks like at the end (~age 39)

Per *What being attracts* in `../I-AM.md`:

- **Body fully integrated.** Yoga practice consistent. Possibly teaching.
- **Wife + family + (probable) child** anchored.
- **Small circle of deep trust** + **wider circle of mutual value-exchange**.
- **Wealth-feeling stable.** Money flows because of what's been created and the trust around it, not because of what's been chased.
- **Place stable** — anchored, not floaty.
- **Multiple creations visible.** Some teaching, some sharing, some tangible things people use. Shape emergent, not forced. Possibly agentic-shaped, possibly not.
- **Day-to-day texture consistent.** The repetition of the day IS the manifestation.

## How this doctrine evolves

This file is **doctrine, not plan**. Updates when:

- A necessary condition is revealed to be missing (during real-life testing).
- The Singer-archetype frame produces friction (live experience contradicts the surrender principle in some specific way).
- The 80/20 blueprint produces friction in either direction (too much for-others starves own-things, or vice versa).

**Reviewed at consolidation cadence** + whenever a major path-decision is on the table. Otherwise: leave alone. The point of this file is to BE STABLE so it can serve as a filter when real choices arrive.
