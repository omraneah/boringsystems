## I AM

The canonical list. Each is a being-statement — already-true, not aspirational, not a target.

### Body / movement
- **I am a mover.**

### State / felt-tone
- **I am calm, serene, peaceful.**
- **I am present.**
- **I am content. I am at peace with what is.**

### Heart
- **I am loving.**
- **I am kind, not nice.** Kindness acts from a loving place even when the truth destabilizes the receiver. Niceness accommodates and is fragile. Firmness lives inside the kindness — it is what makes the kindness real.
- **I am relational.** I exist through mutual exchange with others — not in a vacuum. The web of relationships is the substrate, not a domain alongside others.

### Will / mode of action
- **I am courageous, not striving.** I act from curiosity, growth, and felt sense — not from hustle, muscle, or fear. If I feel something, I act on it.
- **I am curious.**

### Sovereignty / freedom
- **I am sovereign.**
- **I am free.** Free *within* a chosen life. Increasingly free of the traumas that used to dictate — they soften as they're seen, not by force.
- **I am rooted.** I do not run from locations. I anchor and expand from where I am. Geography itself doesn't matter; the *posture* of being rooted does. The medium-term application is France / Paris; the being-statement is location-independent.

### Wealth
- **I am wealthy.** I do not act from scarcity. I am not driven by scarcity. I am not afraid of money.

### Creator
- **I am a creator.** I express in ways that add value to the world — teaching, sharing, building tangible things people use. **The creation-shape stays open by design.** I refuse to pre-commit to a project, domain, or vehicle. Singer is the model — yoga, construction, a software company all in one life, none of them planned, each entered when life put it in front of him. The shape follows the being; the being does not bend to the shape. Exchange of value with the world will happen — but it's downstream of creation, not its goal.

## The Why (one phrase per being)

- **Mover** — because the body is the compass. When the mover goes quiet, the compass goes dark, and life mistakes follow.
- **Calm, serene, peaceful** — because the felt baseline determines what I attract and how clearly I see. Pressure narrows; calm widens.
- **Present** — because life is too short to live anywhere else. The moment is the only thing real. Living in the moment is not a journey or a state to force — it is a non-state I return to by removing what blocks it.
- **Content / at peace with what is** — because grasping for what isn't ruins what already is.
- **Loving / kind not nice** — because love that won't say the destabilizing truth isn't love. Niceness is fragile; kindness is real.
- **Relational** — because what I give to the world flows through others; what compounds for me flows back through them. Network is not a domain — it is the medium. Relationships are the substrate of any net-worth worth caring about.
- **Courageous, not striving** — because life is too short to live it in suffering. Curiosity-driven courage compounds; hustle-driven striving burns.
- **Curious** — because curiosity is the texture of being alive.
- **Sovereign** — because freedom is what I am, not a goal. Anything that captures me deteriorates me.
- **Free** — because I refuse to be slave to money, to perishable pleasures, to digital noise, to hustle culture, to my own past traumas.
- **Rooted** — because what compounds, compounds in place. Running between locations is a way to never let anything take root, including me. Anchored, I expand. Floating, I dissipate.
- **Wealthy / not from scarcity** — because acting from scarcity is the failure mode that makes everything else collapse. Wealth is felt-state, not bank balance.
- **Creator** — because expression is the natural overflow of being lived. The work is a way of living, not what I do for a living.

## Relational architecture — network as net worth

Relationships are not a domain alongside others; they are the substrate. **Network = net worth**, in any sense of "worth" worth caring about. I exist through mutual exchange with others — not in a vacuum. The web is the medium through which everything else moves.

**Anti-fragility is the lens.** A relationship that breaks under truth was never real. The ones that compound through truth — the destabilizing moment, the corrected friction, the honest no — are the ones I cultivate. The willingness to exit early is what makes commitment safe.

**No extraction, either direction.** Not from me, not from them. The moment a relationship becomes one-sided, it has stopped being a relationship and started being a transaction. I correct the imbalance or I exit. Pure giving is as off as pure taking.

**See the end before the beginning crystallizes.** Toxicity is felt early — usually before the other person sees it. When the felt-signal is there, I stop. No short-termism, no "let's see if it gets better," no extraction in the meantime. The willingness to see the end early is what makes the beginning safe.

**Diversity is required.** Small-deep + wider-mutual + connector-routing — three layers active simultaneously. Monoculture is fragility by design; multi-layer is anti-fragile by design.

**The non-negotiable.** Every direction of work, every project, every commitment runs through this lens. *Is this relationship anti-fragile, diverse, reciprocated, non-extractive?* — upstream of "is the work good." A "good" engagement with a wrong-shaped relationship is wrong. The relationship dimension is load-bearing for everything else.

## Day-to-day texture — the lived projection

Not a vision board. Not a forecast. Not an "energetic representation built vividly in the head until it becomes inevitable" — that visualization-as-manifestation move is not how I work. The way I project is by attending to the felt-shape of a day where the being is fully lived, and letting life arrange the rest. The repetition of the day IS the manifestation.

**Morning.** Wake energized, on my own terms. No hurry. Coffee, meditation, sunlight, tranquility. Start calmly. Feel enthusiastic about what's coming next.

**Midday.** Move into creation — whatever shape it takes that day. Deep work blocks. Connection with nature and movement during the day. Loving moments with the people who matter most: wife, family, hopefully a bigger family with a kid. Reach out to the world — not in a bubble, but not forcing either. Talking to deeply connected, smart people.

**Closing.** Cool down smoothly. Eat well, move well. Not stressed about pleasing others, controlling them, or changing myself so I resonate better with them. Chilled-out time with wife and family. Sleep early. Serene mind.

**Throughout.** Self-anchored, not externally anchored. Acting from kindness. Not reactive to noise. Appreciative — for life, for the country I'm in, for the people around me, for society, for what I'm creating, for how the world interacts with that.

The day repeats this way. Tomorrow same. Year after, same texture, different details. Concreteness on what gets created intentionally absent — the texture is the projection, not the inventory.

## Stable preferences — guardrails that don't change with being

These are not aspirational. They are durable felt-preferences that hold across chapters.

- **Nature.** Daily / weekly contact. Nature is constitutional, not a wellness add-on.
- **Depth.** No surface in ideas, relationships, work, or places.
- **Body-first navigation.** Yoga, movement, intuitive felt-sense as primary compass.
- **Resonance, not persuasion.** I attract through coherence. Working harder to convince is the inverse failure mode.
- **Silence over justification in high stakes.** Justification kills leverage; silence holds it.
- **Sensory low-load.** Quiet over loud. Soft light over harsh. The body needs space, not stimulation.
- **Slow tempo.** Default operating pace is slow. Fast is the exception, deliberately chosen, never the default.
- **Solo-default while building trust toward chosen partnership.** Default to operating solo while testing for trust bit by bit. Open — actively open — to deep partnership with the right person. **Not** a lone-wolf identity (that belief is outdated and being moved beyond), but a risk-management posture that buys the time needed for trust to form. The chosen interdependence is the direction; the solo-default is the bridge.
- **Connection-loving from an anchored place.** Within and beyond the solo-default: I love working with others, sharing, adding value, building things together. The qualifier is the *from-where* — anchored, kind, assertive, never from need-to-please or need-to-control. When the trust is real, I lean in fully.
- **English as primary register.** EN is the anchor for thinking and writing. FR is context — held fluently, not preferred for inner-game.
- **Minimalist, simple.** Aesthetic across spaces, work, and things. Less, but better.
- **Improving only when it makes sense.** If it works, it works. Tweaking for the sake of tweaking is the perfectionist failure mode — I refuse it. Acceptance of "good enough" is part of the wealth-feeling.
- **Serenity, alone or with others, in nature or not.** The felt-state of serenity is location-independent and company-independent. Quiet enough to hear myself, present enough to be in the room. Not a private retreat — a baseline I carry across contexts.

## Kindness ≠ Niceness

This deserves its own clarification because the distinction is load-bearing.

**Niceness:** accommodates the receiver. Avoids saying things that might destabilize. Is fragile — a niceness that breaks under pressure was never love.

**Kindness:** acts from a loving place. Says what is true and useful, even when it doesn't feel good in the moment. The receiver may be seeking something else and the kindness may not feel kind to them in that moment — that's fine. Kindness is calibrated to the long arc of love, not to the short arc of momentary comfort.

Firmness is not separate from kindness. **Firmness lives inside the kindness — it is what makes the kindness safe and real.** A kindness without firmness is niceness with better packaging.
