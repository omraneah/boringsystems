---
name: Propose title options for work case files before committing
description: Work case file titles require 3–5 options proposed to Ahmed before the title is applied — functional defaults are not acceptable.
type: feedback
---

For every new work case file, propose 3–5 title options explicitly before writing and committing the file. Do not apply a functional/descriptive title without giving Ahmed a choice.

**Why:** Ahmed has a specific editorial sensibility for this series. The work series titles are short and punchy: "SaaS Hardening", "Breaking Vendor Lock-In", "Architecture Governance". A default descriptive title wastes a revision cycle and signals the author didn't engage with the editorial register of the series.

**How to apply:** After drafting the article content, output a "Title options:" block with 3–5 alternatives before creating the file. The functional/descriptive title is option A. Propose 2–4 alternatives that match the series register (short, action-oriented or noun-phrase, no jargon overload). Wait for Ahmed to pick one before committing. Same applies to writing articles if a title change is ever proposed mid-draft.
