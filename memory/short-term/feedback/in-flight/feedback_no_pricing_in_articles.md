name: No pricing figures in articles
description: Articles must never include specific pricing numbers — they become stale and undermine the site's long-term credibility

---

Never include specific pricing figures ($/month, $/connection, annual totals, plan tiers with dollar amounts) in published articles.

**Why:** Pricing changes on short timescales. Articles are meant to stay consistent and credible over years. A specific number that's wrong in six months undermines the whole piece — and signals the author didn't think about shelf life.

**How to apply:** Describe pricing structure or model (per-connection, MAU-based, free core + enterprise tier, OSS) but never specific dollar amounts. If calibration context is genuinely useful, use order-of-magnitude brackets ("hundreds per month", "four figures annually for large teams") — never exact numbers. This applies to all writing-en, writing-fr, work-en, work-fr content. When reviewing existing articles, strip specific pricing figures during any revision pass.
