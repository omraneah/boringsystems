## Singer practice — first codification round

Ahmed reported a sustained deepening into Michael Singer's work over recent weeks. Practice has crystallized enough to name. Today: first codification round. Many more expected.

### What's new in the practice

- **"I can handle it"** — directive line. Runs when something arises. Names what is already true (capacity is present).
- **"Resistance doesn't fix anything"** — diagnostic.
- **"Regardless of my preferences"** — preferences don't gate acceptance.
- **"I am capable of accepting reality"** — being-statement the practice produces; maturation of I-AM "I am at peace with what is."
- **Surrender = inside work** — let resistance pass through → clarity emerges → action chosen from clarity. NOT outer passivity / doormat.
- **3-second pause before responding** to anyone or anything — universal protocol (personal, work, chat).
- **Signal vs. noise** — signal = pull from beyond self-inflicted preferences; noise = preference-engine, validation-engine, prove-something-engine.
- **Reduced external exposure** as deliberate practice (current phase). Not seclusion. Singer didn't seclude.
- **Personal-mind vs. outward curiosity** — focusing on outside richness breaks the self-rumination loop.
