**Who.** Rémi Alvado, French ex-CTO/CPO. Twenty-year career anchored at WIZBII (10 years), Kelkoo, BestOfMedia. Operates a consulting practice at `remi.alva.do/prestation/*` — in market roughly 6 months as of 2026-04-25, found a stabilizing niche.

**Surface.** Five separate `/prestation/X` pages, French-only:
- `/formation-ia-agentique` — IA training (sur-mesure or pre-built tracks)
- `/accompagnement-pme` — PME modernization / digitalization (importing tech-ecosystem methods to SMEs)
- `/sprint-fondateur` — 2-month MVP + business plan + investor deck for non-technical founders
- `/management-de-transition` — Interim CTO/CPO during a leadership vacuum
- `/creation-equipe-technique` — Building an internal tech team (post-MVP, exiting agency/no-code)

**What he does (pattern across the five):**
- Formal "vous", operator-to-operator hybrid register, never "tu".
- Heavy English business/tech terms preserved in French prose (No-Code, Growth, Stack Techno, DevOps, MVP, PRD, JEI, CIR — the BPI funding angle is a France-specific lever).
- Each page opens with a buyer-side problem statement (diagnosis-before-solution).
- Methodology in numbered phases per engagement.
- Credibility anchor: WIZBII / Kelkoo / BestOfMedia named in nearly every page.
- Subtle CTA: *"Prenons 30 minutes pour cadrer votre besoin"* → `/rendez-vous`. No booking calendar visible.
- No testimonials, no client logos, no FAQ, no pricing on most pages.
- Sprint Fondateur references *"équivalent à 2 mois d'un CTO senior fractional"* and an explicit minimum threshold (15K€ noted as insufficient).
- Sprint Fondateur uses a fictional case study (`Bouch.ee`) as pedagogical example.
- Accompagnement PME mentions BPI Innovation Axes Diagnostic 50% co-pay support.

**What he's optimized for.** Cold inbound. A stranger from search or referral lands on a single `/prestation/X` page, evaluates him in 60 seconds, books a 30-min call. The five-page-split + per-page methodology + named past companies + free diagnostic CTA = a conversion funnel for cold strangers.

**What Ahmed deliberately did NOT copy (decision recorded in DECISIONS.md, 2026-04-25):**
- One page with all four shapes inline, not five separate pages.
- No SEO meta on the page, not in main nav. Sent only when asked in conversation.
- No credibility anchor in the engagement-shapes page itself (the About page carries the bio).
- No "30-min call" CTA, no booking calendar.
- No fictional case studies. The Building-lane pieces (`/en/building/*`) are the actual proof, discoverable separately.
- No phase-by-phase methodology breakdowns — board's "harness-piece register, declarative not brochure" was explicit.

**What Ahmed DID adopt from the comparison:**
- "When it doesn't fit" line per shape (Alvado's Sprint Fondateur has an explicit disqualification section; Godin's "wrong people bounce" criterion validated it; cheap to add, sharpens the filter).

**When this reference is useful again.**
